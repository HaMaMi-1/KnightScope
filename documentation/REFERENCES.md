# Academic References & Bibliography

1. **Shannon, Claude E.** (1950). *Programming a Computer for Playing Chess*. Philosophical Magazine, Series 7, Vol. 41, No. 314, pp. 256–275.
   - Seminal paper formalizing the distinction between Type A (brute-force minimax) and Type B (heuristic selective search) chess strategies.

2. **Knuth, Donald E., & Moore, Ronald W.** (1975). *An Analysis of Alpha-Beta Pruning*. Artificial Intelligence, Vol. 6, No. 4, pp. 293–326.
   - Mathematical proof demonstrating that optimal move ordering reduces the effective branching factor of search from $b$ to $b^{1/2}$, effectively doubling the solvable search depth.

3. **Korf, Richard E.** (1985). *Depth-First Iterative-Deepening: An Optimal Admissible Tree Search*. Artificial Intelligence, Vol. 27, No. 1, pp. 97–109.
   - Foundational formulation of Iterative Deepening Search proving asymptotic optimality in time and space complexity for adversarial and single-agent search.

4. **Russell, Stuart, & Norvig, Peter.** (2020). *Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach* (4th ed.). Pearson.
   - Comprehensive reference text on adversarial game search, game formulation, state spaces, transition models, and minimax/alpha-beta heuristics.

5. **Campbell, Murray, Hoane, A. Joseph, & Hsu, Feng-hsiung.** (2002). *Deep Blue*. Artificial Intelligence, Vol. 134, Iss. 1–2, pp. 57–83.
   - Architectural and algorithmic discussion of specialized search, piece-square evaluations, and search enhancements.

6. **Michniewski, Tomasz.** (2014). *Simplified Evaluation Function*. Chess Programming Wiki (CPW).
   - Standard piece-square tables (PST) and linear piecewise positional evaluation heuristics adopted in educational chess systems.

7. **Finkelstein, Lev, & Markovitch, Shaul.** (1998). *Learning to Play Chess Using Heuristic Search and Selective Deepening*. Annals of Mathematics and Artificial Intelligence, Vol. 24, pp. 223–242.
   - Comparative analysis of heuristic lookahead evaluation vs greedy single-step decision making in complex zero-sum state spaces.

8. **Fierz, Martin.** (2001). *Strategy and Search in Computer Chess: The Architecture of Chess Engines*. International Computer Games Association (ICGA) Journal, Vol. 24, No. 2, pp. 71–84.
   - Systematic treatment of move ordering techniques (MVV-LVA, killer move heuristic) and evaluation balancing in software implementations.
